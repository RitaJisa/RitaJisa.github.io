<h2 style="color: #5777C7; border-bottom: 2px solid #5777C7; padding-bottom: 5px;">Professional Biography</h2>

Dr. Rita Margarita Jiménez Sánchez holds a Ph.D. and an M.A. in Social Sciences with a specialization in Rural Studies from El Colegio de Michoacán (COLMICH), as well as a B.A. in Intercultural Development and Management from the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM). 

Her research trajectory lies at the intersection of political anthropology, contemporary intellectual history, and critical discourse analysis. She focuses on the political uses of identity, the formation of structural and gender-based violence, and access to justice. In recognition of her academic rigor, she was awarded the 2022 Arturo Warman Prize for the best doctoral dissertation on the political uses of indigenous identity.

With over a decade of experience, Dr. Jiménez Sánchez effectively bridges qualitative research and ethnographic fieldwork with policy design, institutional evaluation, and community engagement.

<h2 style="color: #5777C7; border-bottom: 2px solid #5777C7; padding-bottom: 5px;">Areas of Expertise & Institutional Impact</h2>

* <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Qualitative & Ethnographic Research:</strong> Extensive experience in field research, participatory community diagnostics, historical anthropology, and critical discourse analysis.
* <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Human Rights & Gender Perspective:</strong> Public policy design, development of evaluation models for Women's Justice Centers (INACIPE), and analysis of structural violence, disappearance, and femicide.
* <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Curriculum Design & Institutional Training:</strong> Specialized training for judicial personnel, prosecutors, and university faculty across major institutions (UNAM, EJEM, INACIPE).
* <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Binational Collaboration & Transnational Research:</strong> External Research Collaborator at the UCSB Labor Center (University of California, Santa Barbara) analyzing agricultural supply chains and labor rights, along with methodological consultations with UNICEF Mexico on armed violence and childhood.

<h2 style="color: #5777C7; border-bottom: 2px solid #5777C7; padding-bottom: 5px;">Resumen Ejecutivo (Versión corta en español)</h2>

La Dra. Rita M. Jiménez Sánchez es Doctora y Maestra en Ciencias Sociales (Estudios Rurales) por El Colegio de Michoacán y Licenciada en Desarrollo y Gestión Interculturales por la UNAM. Fue galardonada con el Premio Arturo Warman 2022 por su investigación doctoral sobre los usos políticos de la identidad indígena.

Su trabajo articuladamente integra la investigación cualitativa y la antropología política con el diseño de políticas públicas e intervenciones institucionales. Se especializa en la prevención de las violencias de género, igualdad sustantiva, derechos humanos y justicia intercultural. Actualmente, colabora como Investigadora Externa en el UCSB Labor Center e impulsa proyectos binacionales entre México y Estados Unidos.
