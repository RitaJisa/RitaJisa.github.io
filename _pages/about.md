<h2 style="color: #5777C7; border-bottom: 2px solid #5777C7; padding-bottom: 5px;">Professional Biography</h2>

Dr. Rita Margarita Jiménez Sánchez holds a Ph.D. and an M.A. in Social Sciences with a specialization in Rural Studies from El Colegio de Michoacán (COLMICH), as well as a B.A. in Intercultural Development and Management from the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM)[cite: 1]. 

Her research trajectory lies at the intersection of political anthropology, contemporary intellectual history, and critical discourse analysis[cite: 1]. She focuses on the political uses of identity, the formation of structural and gender-based violence, and access to justice[cite: 1]. In recognition of her academic rigor, she was awarded the <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">2022 Arturo Warman Prize</strong> for the best doctoral dissertation on the political uses of indigenous identity[cite: 1].

With over a decade of experience, Dr. Jiménez Sánchez effectively bridges qualitative research and ethnographic fieldwork with policy design, institutional evaluation, and community engagement[cite: 1].

<h2 style="color: #5777C7; border-bottom: 2px solid #5777C7; padding-bottom: 5px;">Areas of Expertise & Institutional Impact</h2>

* <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Qualitative & Ethnographic Research:</strong> Extensive experience in field research, participatory community diagnostics, historical anthropology, and critical discourse analysis[cite: 1].
* <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Human Rights & Gender Perspective:</strong> Public policy design, development of evaluation models for Women's Justice Centers (INACIPE), and analysis of structural violence, disappearance, and femicide[cite: 1].
* <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Curriculum Design & Institutional Training:</strong> Specialized training for judicial personnel, prosecutors, and university faculty across major institutions (UNAM, EJEM, INACIPE)[cite: 1].
* <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Binational Collaboration & Transnational Research:</strong> External Research Collaborator at the UCSB Labor Center (University of California, Santa Barbara) analyzing agricultural supply chains and labor rights[cite: 1], along with methodological consultations with UNICEF Mexico on armed violence and childhood[cite: 1].

<h2 style="color: #5777C7; border-bottom: 2px solid #5777C7; padding-bottom: 5px;">Resumen Ejecutivo (Versión corta en español)</h2>

La <strong style="color: #6F57C7;">Dra. Rita M. Jiménez Sánchez</strong> es Doctora y Maestra en Ciencias Sociales (Estudios Rurales) por El Colegio de Michoacán y Licenciada en Desarrollo y Gestión Interculturales por la UNAM[cite: 1]. Fue galardonada con el Premio Arturo Warman 2022 por su investigación doctoral sobre los usos políticos de la identidad indígena[cite: 1].

Su trabajo articuladamente integra la investigación cualitativa y la antropología política con el diseño de políticas públicas e intervenciones institucionales[cite: 1]. Se especializa en la prevención de las violencias de género, igualdad sustantiva, derechos humanos y justicia intercultural[cite: 1]. Actualmente, colabora como Investigadora Externa en el UCSB Labor Center e impulsa proyectos binacionales entre México y Estados Unidos[cite: 1].
